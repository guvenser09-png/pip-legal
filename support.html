Common questions

Is Pip really free?

Yes, permanently. Unlimited brain dumps and three start-togethers a day cost nothing and always will. Pip Pro adds unlimited starts, all the pip friends and hats, and calendar sync when it ships.

How do I cancel Pip Pro?

iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Pip → Cancel. Cancelling during the 14-day trial costs you nothing. Apple handles all billing; we cannot see or charge your card.

I paid but Pro isn't showing.

Open Pip → Settings → Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us and we'll sort it out.

Can I get my data back after deleting the app?

No, and we want to be upfront about it: everything lives only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes the data. That is the trade for Pip having no account and no server.

Is Pip a medical app?

No. Pip is a productivity tool built with executive-function-friendly patterns. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a substitute for care from a professional.
